Schematic cell portraits, not to scale. T-cell badges identify subtypes; their shapes alone do not distinguish them. The paired memory symbol represents both B and T cells.

How accurate is this? This is a simplified teaching map of selected immune pathways. Connections include signalling, differentiation and protein production; some act through intermediate cells that are not drawn. Scenario timings are illustrative and vary with dose, route, age and prior exposure. Selected entries include sources; the full atlas has not yet had a systematic evidence review.

Simplifications worth naming: innate lymphoid cells, γδ T cells, follicular dendritic cells, basophils and the bone marrow compartment are described in the text but not drawn. The germinal centre is compressed into a single B cell node. Positions show representative compartments, not exclusive locations: cells migrate, and effector cells also act outside lymph nodes.
